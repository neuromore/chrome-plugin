How to control your browser with neurofeedback

1. Open a website

Open a website of your choice, for example one of the sites below, then reopen this extension on that page.

  • Netflix
  • YouTube
  • Prime Video
  • Spotify
  • Apple Music

2. Connect to neuromore Studio

Open neuromore Studio, then click Connect to link it to this tab.

3. Run a biofeedback session

Start a session in the Studio and observe the brightness and volume changing in your browser.
